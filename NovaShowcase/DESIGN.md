## Style Prompt
Swiss Pulse: Black and near-black canvas with one electric blue accent. Grid-locked compositions where every element snaps to an invisible 12-column grid. Numbers dominate, counters animate from zero. Hard cuts or geometric transitions. Nothing decorative.

## Colors
- Background: #0a0a0f (near-black, blue-tinted)
- Foreground: #ffffff (pure white for headlines)
- Accent: #0066FF (electric blue)
- Secondary: #8888aa (muted blue-grey for labels)
- Surface: #14141f (dark card backgrounds)

## Typography
- Headlines: Inter Bold, 700-900 weight, 60-120px
- Body/Labels: Inter Regular, 400 weight, 20-28px
- Numbers: Inter Bold, tabular-nums, 80-120px

## Motion
- expo.out, power3.out for entrances
- Fast entries (0.3-0.6s), snap into place
- Offset staggered entrances by 0.15-0.3s
- Nothing floats, nothing drifts

## What NOT to Do
- No gradient text
- No decorative glow effects on text
- No centered-everything layouts
- No floating/orbiting elements
- No slow fades - be precise and fast
- No purple, cyan, or neon colors
